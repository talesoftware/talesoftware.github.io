Privacy Policy

“Curse of Erbu RPG” (Android) · effective 8 November 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is TaleSoftware – Kamil Burza.
Contact for all privacy matters: talesoftwarestudio@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Save games, settings and pictures taken in photo mode are stored only on your device (pictures in the gallery, in the “Erbu” folder). We never upload them and have no access to them.

3. Statistics and crash reports – only with your consent

To fix bugs and make the game run smoothly on different phones, the game may send anonymous statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics). This happens only after you give consent on first launch. You can withdraw consent at any time in the game Settings. Game versions without the consent screen send no data at all.

Data typeExamples
Installation IDa random identifier assigned by Firebase, not linked to your name or account
Device informationphone model, Android version, language, approximate country
In-game activityquests started and completed, play time, island area, game version
Performanceframes per second, frame time, chosen graphics settings
Crash reportslocation of the error in the game code, memory state at the time of the crash

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, precise location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. The game contains no ads.

Purpose: finding and fixing bugs, improving performance, learning which parts of the game are too hard or unclear.
Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
Retention: statistics – 2 months, crash reports – 90 days.

4. Buying the full version

The full version is purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google – we never receive your card details or address. We only receive a purchase confirmation needed to unlock the full version (legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Google Play Games services

If the game offers achievements or cloud saves and you sign in to Google Play Games, that data (e.g. unlocked achievements, the save file) is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional – you can play without it.

6. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA

The data described in section 3 is processed on our behalf by Google Ireland Limited (the Firebase provider). It may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Deleting your data: turn statistics off in the game Settings and e-mail talesoftwarestudio@gmail.com with the installation ID shown in Settings. We will delete the related data within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes saves and settings from your phone.

8. Children

The game contains fantasy violence and is not directed at children. Users under 16 should only agree to statistics with the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have received a child’s data without such consent, we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Changes to this policy

We will announce significant changes on this page and – if they affect what data is collected – in the game, asking for your consent again.
